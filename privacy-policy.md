# Privacy Policy

**Animated Month Wallpaper**  
Last updated: 27 March 2026

## Overview
Animated Month Wallpaper is a simple animated wallpaper app that displays the current month name on your screen. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Data Collection
This app does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We do not collect:
- Personal information
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- Usage analytics
- Any other data

## Local Storage
The app saves your personal preferences (such as chosen colours, fonts, and language) locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

## Internet Access
The app requests internet permission solely to load display fonts from Google Fonts on first launch. No personal data is sent or received.

## Third Party Services
The app uses Google Fonts for typography. Please refer to Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy for information on how Google handles font requests.

## Children's Privacy
This app does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy
Any changes to this privacy policy will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
